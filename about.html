Why we started Can Boys

We noticed that dirty trash cans were a common problem that often got overlooked. They build up grime quickly, start to smell, and are one of those things most people simply do not want to clean themselves. We also saw that many existing cleaning services charged more than the job seemed worth.

We started Can Boys to offer a simpler option for people in Manhattan Beach: reliable service, thorough cleaning, and fair pricing. We work around trash pickup so the process is easy for customers, and we focus on doing the job well without making it more complicated than it needs to be.
